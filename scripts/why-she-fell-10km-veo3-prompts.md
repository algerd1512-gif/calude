# Veo 3 prompts — Why A Woman Fell 10 Kilometers From The Sky And Survived

IMPORTANT PRODUCTION NOTE: Wherever Vesna Vulovic appears as a visible person (not just referenced in narration), the prompt renders her as a **stylized 3D-model / simulation figure** (matte clay-render or low-poly mannequin look, featureless or softly abstracted face) instead of a photorealistic human likeness. This is deliberate: Veo 3 cannot hold one consistent human face across dozens of separately generated 8-second clips, so a photoreal "Vesna" would look like a different woman in every shot. A stylized simulation figure reads as consistent, intentional, and avoids that failure mode entirely. Every such prompt below is marked **[3D-SIM FIGURE]**. All other prompts default to the channel's normal restrained photorealistic documentary style.

Every prompt ends with: "No text, no captions, no watermark." Additional exclusions noted where relevant.
