## Description (copy-paste ready)

In January 1972, a 22-year-old flight attendant fell 10,160 meters — higher than the summit of Mount Everest — after a bomb tore her plane apart in mid-air over Czechoslovakia. She survived. It remains, more than 50 years later, the highest fall any human has ever survived without a parachute.

Vesna Vulović was working JAT Yugoslav Airlines Flight 367 from Stockholm to Belgrade on January 26, 1972, when a bomb hidden in the cargo hold detonated at 33,330 feet, ripping the DC-9 into three pieces. Trapped inside the tumbling tail section, pinned by a food and drinks trolley, she fell for roughly three minutes before the wreckage came down on a snow-covered forested hillside. She spent 27 days in a coma, was paralyzed for months, and in 1985 was awarded the Guinness World Record for the highest fall survived without a parachute — handed to her personally by Paul McCartney. No one has come close to it since.
